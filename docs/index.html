Distant islands and territories (such as Hawaii) are left out of the outline you drag. Your scores stay in this browser.

How to play

Most world maps use the Mercator projection. It keeps shapes right but stretches everything near the poles: Greenland looks as big as Africa, but Africa is about 14 times bigger.

Each round gives you an orange country to place on a blue one, on a Mercator map. Drag it over and resize it until it would be the right size if it really sat there. Then lock in.

The Equal Earth map then shows the true sizes side by side. You score 100 for a perfect size and 0 if you're about 3 times too big or too small (in area). 🟩 is within about 10%, 🟨 within about 30%.

"Mercator fooled me" is how far you stayed toward the size Mercator suggests, averaged over the rounds where Mercator lies by at least 1.5×.

Daily: five rounds, the same for everyone, new at midnight UTC. Practice: as many as you like. Explore: tap countries to compare them on both maps.
